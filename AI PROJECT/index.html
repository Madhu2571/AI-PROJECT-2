Field Study Background: Salem Uzhavar Sandhai, Dadagapatti

The Uzhavar Sandhai (Farmers' Market) at Dadagapatti, Salem was established to eliminate intermediary middlemen by providing direct marketing infrastructure for vegetable and fruit cultivators. However, smallholder vendors face multi-dimensional daily trade-offs:

  • Time Windows: Uzhavar Sandhai operates in an early morning window (05:00 AM – 11:30 AM). Unsold stock faces severe distress pricing or transport back home.
  • Transport vs Margin: Traveling to distant wholesale mandis (e.g. Attur or Leigh Bazaar) incurs higher transport costs and transit heat spoilage, even if buyer absorption capacity is higher.
  • Weather Sensitivity: Sudden showers or excessive midday heat rapidly degrades perishable vine crops like tomatoes and greens.

Gradient Boosting Regression

Used for short-term price and arrival volume estimation. Models multi-variate non-linear interactions between historical mandi arrivals, seasonal cycles, day-of-week demand patterns, and rainfall indicators.

Multi-Criteria Ranking Algorithm

Evaluates selling destinations across conflicting objectives using weighted utility theory (AHP / TOPSIS). Balances expected net monetary return against transit distance, perishable transit spoilage risk, and demand absorption speed.
